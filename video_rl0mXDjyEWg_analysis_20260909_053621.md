### Explicit Rules Extracted

**1. Qualifying Displacement**
*   Displacement is strictly recognized and defined by the presence of a Fair Value Gap (FVG) that forms as a result of the price moving away from the order block. Without an FVG, it does not qualify as a "High Probability" order block.

**2. Last Opposing Candle (Creation of the OB)**
*   The speaker defines the creation of the order block by looking at the "previous move".
*   The creating candle must feature a "wick or a move above the high or below the low of the previous move."
*   There must be a candle body *close* above/below the *opening price* of that previous move. 

**3. Zone Boundaries**
*   The primary zone interaction is the entire order block candle. 
*   The specific, mathematically critical boundary is the **Mean Threshold**, which the speaker defines strictly as the exact 50% midpoint of the candle's *body* (measured from the opening price to the closing price, explicitly ignoring the wicks for this specific calculation).

**4. Confirmation**
*   To upgrade a "Standard" order block to a "High Probability" order block, two confirmation criteria must be met after the block is formed:
    1.  A Fair Value Gap (FVG) must be created (Displacement).
    2.  A Break of Structure (BOS) or a Market Structure Shift (MSS) must occur. 

**5. Mitigation / Retest**
*   Entry is placed directly at the order block or the accompanying Fair Value Gap when price returns to (taps into) the zone. 
*   Price is permitted to wick into the order block, and wick past the mean threshold, as long as it does not close past it.

**6. Invalidation**
*   An order block is considered invalid (the trade is "null and void" or "likely to fail") if a subsequent candle *body closes* past the Mean Threshold. 
*   Wicks extending past the Mean Threshold do not invalidate the setup, but body closes strictly do.

**7. Timeframe**
*   The speaker explicitly states this is not a one-timeframe concept. It is operationally defined as fractal. The video demonstrates the exact same rules applied across the Weekly, Daily, 1-Hour, 15-Minute, and 5-Minute charts.
